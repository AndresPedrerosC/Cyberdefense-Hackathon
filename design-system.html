Principles

  • 1Flat. Use a solid fill and spacing to separate content. Use a full-width rule before a container. No borders around content.
  • 2Evidence required. No finding renders without a source link.
  • 3Match claim strength. Solid tags for proven status only. Weaker claims are dot labels.
  • 4One primary action per view. It is the only bone-filled control on the screen.
  • 5Machine values are mono. Versions, IDs, enum values, and timestamps. Prose is Geist.
  • 6Motion is functional. A row opens, a new finding flashes once. Nothing animates without a state change.
